HOW I PLAY

I have two peculiarities to explain, plus one request.

My request: please do not help me with the puzzles while I am solving them, unless I specifically ask for a hint. I am very happy to discuss them afterward, but I strongly prefer to receive no help, hints, or suggestions during the solve.

First, for variety, in Wordle, Symble, Squidwordle, and similar games, I begin each solve with yesterday’s penultimate guess. I do not like to use the previous day's answer because of its popularity, but I especially like the variety the rule gives.

Second, not only do I play Wordle in Hard Mode, but I have also implemented a personal ruleset that mimics Hard Mode in Symble. Every guess must be a possible answer and must use all information gained so far. That includes placing known characters correctly and using only letters that remain legal under my current interpretation of each symbol.

PERSONAL GOALS

For Wordle, I am happy just to finish, though I start getting nervous after four guesses.

For Symble, I try to solve it in three guesses. I am not disappointed by a four or anything else; three is simply what I aim for.

For the Mini, from Sunday through Friday, my goal is to finish in under one minute, though I do not consider that necessary for a good solve. My personal best is absurdly fast. On Saturday, I relax that goal because the puzzle is larger.

ABOUT SQUIDWORDLE

Squidwordle can overwhelm you with possibilities because the only screening I performed was: “Are you five letters?” If not, the word was removed from the list. If so, it stayed.

That means simple plurals and basic tense variants can appear and become stumbling blocks. This is compounded by recognizing that an S at the end is not merely possible—it is a serious threat.
